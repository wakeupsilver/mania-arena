THE RULEBOOK

Checkers on an 8×8 ring. Pieces move diagonally forward one square. Jump an enemy piece to eliminate it. Jumps are mandatory, and you keep jumping while you can. Reach the far side to become a KING 👑, which can move and jump backward too.

You win when your opponent has no pieces left or no legal moves. If 80 moves pass with no eliminations, whoever has more material (kings count double) wins.

THE ROYAL RUMBLE (Juiced & MAXIMUM)

Every 10 moves, whoever has fewer pieces gets a NEW ENTRANT: a fresh piece runs into one of their back two rows. Each player can get up to 4 entrants. No lead is safe.

HYPE & SPECIAL MOVES

You earn hype by moving, eliminating pieces, crowning kings and getting beaten up. Spend it before you move. Each move can be used once per turn, and you can combine them.

🪑 STEEL CHAIR30Eliminate an enemy (non-king) piece diagonally next to one of yours.
🚪 RUN-IN40A new piece of yours enters an empty square in your back two rows.
🤝 TAG TEAM45Make two moves this turn.
👑 CORONATION55Crown any of your pieces king on the spot.
💥 FROG SPLASH100Your next move lands with a splash: every enemy piece diagonally next to the landing square is eliminated, kings included.

RING EVENTS (MAXIMUM only)

🪑 Chair Shower gives both players +30 hype · 🎆 Pyro blows up one random non-king piece per side · 🌑 Blackout makes every piece look the same for 2 moves · 👑 Title Shot crowns one random piece per side · 🚪 Battle Royal sends a new entrant in for both sides.
