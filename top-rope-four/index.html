THE RULEBOOK

Drop your wrestlers off the top rope into a 7×6 ring. They fall to the lowest open spot.

PINFALLS & CHAIN REACTIONS

Line up 4 or more of your pieces in a row, column or diagonal for a PINFALL. Those pieces leave the ring and everything above them falls down. If the falling pieces make new lines, those are pinfalls too, and they can go to either player. That's a chain reaction.

First to 3 pinfalls wins the title. If both players reach 3 on the same drop, whoever caused it wins. If the ring fills up, most pinfalls wins.

HYPE & SPECIAL MOVES (Juiced & MAXIMUM)

You earn hype by dropping, scoring pinfalls (more for chain reactions) and getting beaten up. Spend it before you drop. Each move can be used once per turn, and you can combine them.

🪑 STEEL CHAIR30Smash any enemy piece. Everything above it falls, which can set off chain reactions.

🙃 PILEDRIVER60Flip the whole ring upside down and let everything fall again.
🏋️ HEAVYWEIGHT100Your next drop crushes every piece in its column and lands on the mat.
